# Independent construction: quadratic and spinor geometry

Read CLAUDE.md, PRD criterion 6/D22--D24, and HANDOFF.md. Root owns shared
files, tracking (`qaag-62k`) and commits. No new subagents. Write only
`scouting/spinor-mechanism-r7.md`. Derive before targeted primary-source
novelty checks. Prefer one or two actual constructions to a catalogue.

Try a genuinely different algorithmic operation arising from quadratic
correspondences, spinor varieties, triality or algebraic norm composition.
The goal is an original quantum mechanism for a concrete geometric classical
output, with a growing input family and charged state/circuit access. A
fixed-dimensional multiplication gadget alone does not supply an advantage.

Root's initial checks to avoid repeating:

- A real polynomial norm identity on product vectors does not make its
  complex-linear coefficient operator a contraction on all quantum inputs.
  Compute its operator norm. For the two-real-coordinate angle-addition map,
  this norm is sqrt(2), giving exact success at most 1/2 at the minimal
  input-copy degree; a balanced fusion tree costs quadratically many sources.
- That angle-sum bit also needs quadratically many total copies under a
  small-common-angle perturbation/Fisher-information comparison, even for
  collective processing. Comparing against separately estimating every
  angle to a worst-case deterministic tolerance would overstate advantage.
- Quaternionic product on unknown real coefficient states is related to
  composing Choi programs with Bell outcomes; correction through an unknown
  operation is not free. Test this reduction instead of naming it new.
- Exterior products, Slater insertion, fermionic Gaussian/matchgate circuits,
  Schur comparison and known programmable filters do not establish D22.
  The campaign already audits their basic use cases.
- Generalized Cayley--Dickson or Jordan constructions need their actual
  positive Hilbert norm, complexification and zero loci. Do not infer a
  dimension-independent probability from an algebraic norm with different
  signature or from a determinant identity.

These are guardrails, not an instruction to prove only negative results.
Derive an explicit physical transformation, coherent register layout or
interaction that goes beyond the listed reductions. State what feature of
the quantum processing is proposed to be original. Then specify a classical
geometric output, resource bound, strongest matched classical attack and a
small hardware diagnostic. If an initial construction collapses, keep the
calculation compact and use remaining effort on a materially different one.
Bound computations and distinguish exact algebraic proofs from diagnostics.
