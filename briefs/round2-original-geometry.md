# Exploration round 2: original collective geometry

Read CLAUDE.md, PRD.md (criterion 6 and D22), HANDOFF.md and definitions conventions.
No web browsing. The user wants original algorithms, not new applications or
substantive equivalents of known algorithms. DQI and extensions, Grover, QFT,
generic spectral filtering, standard SWAP/Schur testing relabelled as a variety
problem, MPS/QSAT relabellings and familiar algorithms are not candidates.

Your bounded task: invent and attack two or three concrete operations and algorithms
involving collective quantum data and algebraic geometry, such as gluing local
algebraic data, compatibility of quantum coordinate charts, nonlinear polynomial
relations across copies, or new geometric outputs. These examples are not endorsed
candidates. Choose your own strongest constructions. Require a nontrivial derived
identity or resource analysis, precise input/output and a matched classical access
model. Do not hide the problem in a state-preparation or measurement oracle.

The other lane handles algebraic transformations/elimination. The root handles
graded-quotient state growth and recycling. Avoid those directions. Do not spawn
agents or edit shared files. Write only scouting/original-geometry-round2.md, about
250 lines. Include exact problem, operation sequence, derived result, complexity,
classical and known-quantum equivalence attacks, physical implementation, and the
smallest missing theorem. End with a MERGE PROPOSAL of provisional definitions and
claims (CONJECTURE or REFUTED only). Historical novelty is unverified.

Scope clarification: algebraic geometry broadly. Existing discoveries here are
optional ingredients, not a restriction; do not limit the search to the Fock or
Macaulay framework or to previously explored quantum-state varieties.

Make a serious constructive attempt but report candidly if no proposal survives.
Send the root agent an early substantive finding before finishing the memo.
