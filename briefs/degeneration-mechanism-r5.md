# Independent construction: degeneration and transport

User authorizes subagents and relentless search across ALL algebraic geometry.
Read CLAUDE.md, PRD criteria 1--6/D22--D24, and HANDOFF for constraints and
completed dispositions. Do not repeat full familiarization or old probes.

Bounded task: derive one or two concrete quantum mechanisms for an intrinsic
AG problem using degeneration, flat families, singularity structure, or another
intrinsic setting you judge better. Choose a strongest explicit construction,
derive its actual operation and resource equation, then attack it. The task is
not a survey or a list of unsolved problems. Send an early substantive formula.

The result must potentially supply an original quantum mechanism, not merely a
new application/encoding/composition of Grover, QFT/HSP/DQI, generic spectral
filtering, LCU/invariant measurement, comparison/discrimination, or known
braid/Hecke/Baxterization algorithms. Basic gates are allowed. Do not mistake
being expressible in a universal gate set for an originality refutation.

State the classical or quantum input precisely, a geometric output, an explicit
nontrivial family, the strongest matched classical attack, and the exact missing
lemma. Charge preparation, normalized operator costs, failure/recovery, readout,
precision and any geometric promise. Existing failures do not prove no new
algorithm exists. Do not manufacture an advantage or historical novelty.

Derive first; use targeted primary-source browsing only to verify the concrete
construction. No web-led scouting. Every shell computation must be bounded by
timeout and one BLAS thread. No subagents. Native collaboration replaces the
old CLI invocation instructions.
