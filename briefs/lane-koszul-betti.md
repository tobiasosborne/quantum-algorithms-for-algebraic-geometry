## The idea (orchestrator's working formulation; attack it as well as develop it)
For a homogeneous ideal I in R = C[z_0..z_n], the graded Betti numbers beta_{i,j}(R/I) = dim Tor_i^R(R/I, C)_j are the homology of the Koszul complex of R/I with respect to the variables. In the Fock model, (R/I)_N is identified (D-inverse-system, §2 rows) with ker H_N = I_N^perp, and I^perp is closed under every annihilation operator a_k = partial_k (since <partial_k g, h> = <g, z_k h>). So on the graded space ker H (x) Lambda(C^{n+1}) (bosonic ground space tensor fermionic exterior algebra) the operator Q = sum_k a_k (x) c_k^dag satisfies Q^2 = 0 EXACTLY (the a_k commute, the c_k^dag anticommute), with no compression needed. The supersymmetric Laplacian L = Q Q^dag + Q^dag Q is block-diagonal in (fermion number i, total boson degree), and by Hodge theory its nullity in the block (i, N) equals a graded Betti number of R/I (fix the exact index convention: which (i,j) of beta_{i,j}, and whether this is Koszul homology of R/I or, by duality, of the inverse system). This gives a Hamiltonian, local in the same sense as H_N (D-k-body), whose ground-state degeneracy per block is the Betti table. No such statement is in the seed or the DAG.
