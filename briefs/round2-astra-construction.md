Read CLAUDE.md, PRD criteria 1--6 and D22--D23, HANDOFF's current steering, and the
openings/dispositions of scouting/original-*-round2.md. No web. No subagents.

The user means a new problem-level result and a new algorithmic mechanism, not a
new-to-the-repo application or an equivalent of a described quantum algorithm.
Grover, QFT, DQI, generic spectral filtering/walks/LCU and other known-algorithm
repackagings are excluded. Basic gates are permitted; the substantive algorithm and
source of advantage must be original. Algebraic geometry broadly is the domain;
existing project discoveries are optional.

exterior adjugates, finite-difference jets, polynomial transduction on copies,
occupation-based tangent-cone extraction, Grassmann wedge intersection, positive
history fusion, or controlled-order birational maps. Their costs/equivalences are
already documented. An independent Astra critic is reviewing that batch separately.

Your bounded subtask is a serious independent construction attempt, rather than a
critic pass: seek one or two mechanisms that are substantively different from that
batch and from established quantum algorithms. You may choose any AG setting.
Derive the central mathematical operation, not just a metaphor or a list of objects.
Specify the computational task, actual input and output, physical operation/circuit,
resource scaling, best classical attack you can derive, and exact missing lemma.
Distinguish an implementable algorithm from an unimplemented oracle/operation.
Do not manufacture a survivor or historical novelty certificate.

Write only scouting/original-astra-round2.md, around 250 lines, with canonical
provisional claims/definitions in a MERGE PROPOSAL. Leave unproved speedup/existence
assertions explicitly held. No shared-file edits. Send an early substantive finding.
