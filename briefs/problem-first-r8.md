# Independent problem-first construction

Read CLAUDE.md, PRD success criteria/D22--D24 and HANDOFF.md. The goal remains
a genuinely original quantum algorithm, not a collection of useful negative
results. Root owns shared files, tracking (`qaag-7kv`) and commits. No new
subagents. Write only `scouting/problem-first-r8.md`.

This is one bounded constructive task: select one concrete computational
bottleneck from classical or modern algebraic geometry, then derive a quantum
procedure that processes its information differently. The prior lanes tended
to start with a geometric operation and then seek an output. Try the reverse:
identify the actual same-input/same-output classical bottleneck first, without
assuming a dense algorithm is the right comparator.

All of algebraic geometry and its applications remain available. Classical
equations, physical quantum data, a streaming model or a succinct circuit can
be used, but the choice and every access asymmetry must be explicit. The
campaign's seed, Fock spaces and tensor varieties are not mandatory.

Send root the first concrete candidate as soon as you have its input/output,
its actual quantum register operation and one governing identity or cost
equation. Do not spend a long turn preparing a broad menu or polishing a
failed toy example. Derive before targeted primary-source checks. An initial
failure should trigger a materially different construction, not a cosmetic
replacement of the geometry.

Required substance for the eventual compact memo:

- The problem and precise classical input/output, with its strongest
  plausible classical algorithm or measurement comparator.
- The proposed quantum operation, state/register semantics and at least
  the first two mathematical obligations on its correctness/resources.
- A ledger including preparation, conditioning, rare outcomes, readout,
  workspace and output precision. Do not compare a quantum state to a
  classical explicit list when the requested output is a bit or sample.
- Exactly which computational result and algorithmic mechanism are claimed
  to be original, and the strongest substantive prior-algorithm reduction.
  Universal-gate compilation is not a novelty objection by itself.
- A small physical diagnostic if the construction survives its first attack.

Previously resolved mechanisms to avoid relaunching: generic Macaulay/QSVT
filters; Grover/QFT/HSP/DQI variants; random-cut/Schur/invariant tests and
known comparison/programmable-discrimination circuits; Bell/GHZ Waring fusion;
inverse jet attenuation; unitary defect completions confused with arithmetic;
reverse multiplication on nilpotent/trinomial algebras; theta-isogeny character
transforms; Hall affine extension sampling and linear-probe commutators;
octonionic reassociation as cubic-phase IQP; folded finite-dimensional
associator filters with matched tomography. Root also derived and then
identified known double-bracket moment-map flow and pure-spinor/Bell Gaussianity
tests; they are in bd memory and should not be presented as fresh mechanisms.

These exclusions do not prove that the broad application domains are closed.
Find a new information-processing step, not just a different object fed to
the same established algorithm. Keep uncertainty explicit and numerical
experiments bounded. A surviving proposal must still face an independent critic.
