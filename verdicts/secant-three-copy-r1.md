# Independent verdict: the three-copy second-secant construction

2026-09-05. Reviewed `argument/secant-three-copy.md`,
`definitions/secant-three-copy.md`, and `checkers/explore/secant_three_copy.py`.
This verdict concerns the manuscript supplied for review, before incorporation
of the random-cut reduction below. It does not review subsequent constructions.

**VERDICT: FAIL as a D22 candidate.** The exact span/projector construction and
fixed-factor-count soundness argument survive mathematical review. However,
ordinary random-cut Schmidt-rank testing gives the same promised testing result
with at most eight times the trials and the same asymptotic gate cost. The
residual itself has a bounded-weight estimator using those established tests.
This is a substantive algorithmic reduction, not a universal-gate compilation
objection. The displayed optimal projector remains a valid exact refinement.

## 1. Mathematical statements that survive

**Cubic span.** The `aab` and `abb` expansions have no alternating local type.
In each standard local representation the three placements give a fixed Weyl
vector times the three real trine vectors. Their common placement label gives
`sum_j v_j^tensor m`, with squared norm `3+6(-1/2)^m`. At `m=1` this is zero.
For the GHZ orbit, the two mixed terms have different local weights, so each
claimed block is nonzero. Irreducibility under the product general-linear group
then fills its Weyl factor, and inequivalent partition tuples separate the
blocks. This proves the displayed span, including its multiplicity line.

**Circuit.** The generalized finite-group extraction in §3.1 follows directly
from matrix-element orthogonality and retains the original representation
coordinate in `b`; it does not provide the entire Weyl basis for free. Its
controlled permutations cost `O(log q_i)`. An alternative explicit extraction
uses a three-cycle's eigenvalues `1,omega,omega^2`, a transposition to identify
the two standard eigenspaces, and a fixed two-dimensional basis rotation.
The eigenvalue-one sector is split into symmetric/alternating types by a
transposition measurement. Both implementations use constant-size group data.

The cat has bond dimension at most three. Its length-`ell` Gram matrix has
diagonal entries one and off-diagonal entries `(-1/2)^ell`; its eigenvalues
are `1+2(-1/2)^ell` and `1-(-1/2)^ell` (twice). Except for the explicitly
rank-deficient endpoints `ell=0,1`, these stay bounded away from zero. Thus
the small-matrix sequential synthesis is effective without hidden exponential
conditioning. The ideal linear gate count and the stated rotation-synthesis
qualification survive. Finite-gate approximation gives bounded two-sided
error, while perfect completeness belongs to the ideal projector.

**Optimality and two-copy obstruction.** Perfect completeness gives
`E phi^3=phi^3`, hence `E>=P_3`. Product basis vectors and pairwise sums of
them give a polarization spanning all of `Sym^2(H)`, so exact one/two-copy
perfect-completeness tests are trivial. The general optimal-span principle is
already [Lovitz--Lowe, Lemma 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.21417v2#S2.SS3.SSS1);
its secant specialization is not an independent new quantum mechanism.

**Soundness.** In §5, local antisymmetry has expectation `e_3(lambda)<=r_3`.
The ordered triple of spectral samples proves `6e_3>=tau_2^2`: after any first
sample a distinct second has probability at least `tau_2`, and after any
distinct pair a new third has probability at least `tau_2`. The commuting
local projection bound and the pure-state trace distance give
`d(psi,phi)<=sqrt(k)(6r_3)^1/4`. Local projection preserves border rank and
increases normalized overlap with states in its range, justifying reduction
to the qubit secant. Replica permutations restrict to the same measurement.

The second-secant ideal is generated by cubic flattening minors, as stated
in [Raicu's primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.5867). Therefore the
compact qubit minimum `g_k(t)` is positive, with the empty-set convention
already supplied. Its certified positive lower bound is computable by real
quantifier elimination. If `r<epsilon^4/(96k^2)`, the projection is nonzero
and lies within `epsilon/2`; the norm triangle inequality gives
`sqrt(g_k(epsilon/2))<=8sqrt(k)r^1/4`. The resulting lower bound
`min{epsilon^4/(96k^2),g_k(epsilon/2)^2/(4096k^2)}` is correct.
Its constants and preprocessing are independent of local dimension only
when `k,epsilon` are fixed; no useful polynomial bound in those parameters
was proved or claimed. The field-versus-Hermitian conjugation conventions
in these statements are consistent.

## 2. Exact random-cut comparison: independent derivation

The following notation is local to this verdict and should enter the sole
definitions source if this comparison is merged. Work on
`G=Sym^3(H)`, equivalently the diagonal-replica-`S_3` invariant subspace.
For a subset `T` of the `k` sites, let

`A_T=(1/6) sum_{pi in S_3} sign(pi) tensor_{i in T} R_i(pi)`

with identity on the other sites. This is the ordinary antisymmetric
projector on three replicas of the subsystem `T`. Its probability on `psi^3`
is the exterior/Schmidt-rank-three probability of that flattening. Draw `T`
by independent fair site bits, including the empty and full subsets, and put

`Abar=2^(-k)sum_T A_T`
`    =(1/6)sum_pi sign(pi) tensor_i ((1+R_i(pi))/2)`.

2.1 Each `A_T` commutes with local isotypic projectors and with the diagonal
`S_3` action. Fix a local-type pattern, let `ell` count alternating sites and
`m` count standard sites, and restrict its multiplicity space to `G`.
Symmetric sites make no difference to the formulas below.

2.2 The identity permutation contributes `1/6` times the identity. For a
three-cycle `c`, the standard representation obeys
`1+R(c)=-R(c)^2`. On the globally invariant sector, the product of its
`R(c)^2` factors is the identity: the symmetric and alternating types are
both trivial on three-cycles. Each of the two cycle terms therefore
contributes `(-1/2)^m/6` times the identity.

2.3 For a transposition `s_j` and a standard site,
`(1+R(s_j))/2=|v_j><v_j|`. If `ell>=1`, a local alternating factor makes
this tensor product zero. If `ell=0`, the sum of the three transposition
contributions is `sum_j |v_j^m><v_j^m|`, with the overall minus sign from
the alternating character.

2.4 Let `u_m=sum_j v_j^m`. On invariant vectors `z`, the three numbers
`<v_j^m,z>` are identical. Thus

`sum_j |v_j^m><v_j^m| z = |u_m><u_m| z /3`
` = (1+2(-1/2)^m) |w_m><w_m| z`.

For `m=0`, interpret all terms as scalars: the same identity reads `3=3`.
For `m=1`, neither the standard representation nor its sign twist has
invariant vectors, so this entire pattern has probability zero on `G`.

2.5 Set `c_m=(1+2(-1/2)^m)/6`. Combining the preceding calculations gives,
on the invariant part of every nonzero pattern,

`Abar = c_m (1-|w_m><w_m|)` if `ell=0`,
`Abar = c_m 1` if `ell>=1`.

The first line is zero at `m=0`. The `m=1` block is absent, so its numerical
`c_1=0` causes no division. For other nonzero blocks, `1/8<=c_m<=1/2`;
`c_0=1/2`, `c_2=1/4`, `c_3=1/8`. Odd `ell` with `m=0` is another absent
invariant block. The second-secant projector excludes every `ell>=1` block.
Consequently, with `Q=1_G-P_3`,

`Q/8 <= Abar|_G <= Q/2`.

These operator inequalities are asserted only on `G`. For arbitrary inputs
outside the identical-copy invariant sector they are not justified by this
argument. For qubits, alternating local types are absent; nonzero rejection
requires `m>=4`, and the sharper constants are `5/32` and `3/16`.

2.6 It follows for every allowed input that

`r_3(psi)/8 <= Pr[random-cut exterior rejection] <= r_3(psi)/2`.

Uniform random-cut testing therefore has perfect completeness and at least
`eta_k(epsilon)/8` soundness under the same geometric-distance promise.
It uses three copies per trial and `O(sum_i log q_i+k)` gates: sample site
bits and perform the constant-group antisymmetry measurement on those
replicas. Repetition costs at most eight times the ideal projector's bound.
This is the established few-copy Schmidt-rank mechanism; the antisymmetry
form is explicitly discussed in
[Lovitz--Lowe §6.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.21417v2#S6.SS2).

2.7 Even additive estimation of the exact `r_3` does not require the cat
measurement. First measure local types, then perform the random-cut test,
obtaining a rejection bit `B`. Return `B/c_m`, with any fixed value on the
zero-probability `m=1` patterns. This variable lies in `[0,8]` and its mean
is exactly `r_3`. Thus ordinary tests estimate the same residual with a
constant concentration overhead. No cancellation, inverse success oracle,
or amortized preparation assumption appears in this reduction.

## 3. Objections, fixes, and surviving statements

### S1 — FATAL to the new-mechanism/north-star claim

**Location:** argument §§1 and 6; any proposed C-row asserting D22 novelty or
an asymptotic improvement over cut-rank testing for this task.
The exact reduction in §2 was missing from the comparison. Noncommutativity
does not force an exponential enumeration of cuts: their uniform average has
a constant gap above precisely the discarded cubic-span space. Being an
optimal projector, rather than literally a convex mixture of cut tests,
does not prevent an algorithmic equivalence at the stated resource scale.
**FIX DEMAND:** Incorporate §2, mark a D22/new-mechanism claim REFUTED, and
withdraw any asymptotic distinction from random-cut testing. Credit the known
optimal-span and antisymmetry frameworks. Keep historical originality of the
specific algebraic identity or circuit implementation explicitly unverified.
**SURVIVING STATEMENT:** The cat circuit implements the exact optimal
three-copy effect. Ordinary random-cut rank tests solve the same promised
decision problem with constant overhead and estimate its residual with bounded
weights. Any single-copy separation for the problem applies to that known
quantum mechanism as well; it cannot establish D22 for the cat construction.

### S2 — MINOR: scope of the implemented effect and compilation bounds

**Location:** argument §3.2's nonsymmetric-input caveat and §§3.4--3.5.
With the exact extraction isometry of §3.1, the retained line is invariant
under the diagonal group. Its pullback range is exactly the span proved in
§2, so the displayed exact effect actually is `P_3`, including its zero
extension. The extra caveat is unnecessary; it should not be confused with
the essential invariant-sector restriction of this verdict's random-cut bound.
**FIX DEMAND:** Remove the unsupported “need not equal” caveat or simply say
the claimed task uses identical pure copies. Preserve the existing distinction
between ideal perfect completeness and synthesized bounded-error gates.
For a concrete failure bound, allocate at most `delta/2` to ideal repetition
and at most `delta/2` to accumulated compilation error.
**SURVIVING STATEMENT:** The representation-coordinate measurement and its
resource ledger are valid; no hidden state-preparation oracle is required.
